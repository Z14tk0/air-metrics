## 🔒 Data Collection

We **do not collect** or store any personal data through the app.

- No name, email, account details, or contact information is collected.
- No personal location data is transmitted to external servers or shared with third parties.
- No health or fitness data is collected.

## 🛠️ Third-Party Services

The app **does not use** any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or telemetry services such as:

- Google Analytics  
- Firebase  
- Crashlytics  
- Ad networks

## 📍 Location Access

The app may request access to your device’s location **to automatically find and display the nearest air quality monitoring station**.

- Location data is processed **locally on your device**.
- Location data is **never stored externally, sold, or shared**.

## 🧠 Permissions Used

The app requests the following permissions for core functionality:

- **Location Access** (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`, `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`): To determine the nearest monitoring station for accurate local air quality and pollen reports.
- **Internet & Network State** (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`): To fetch public air quality index (AQI) measurements and pollen forecast data.
- **Notifications** (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`): To deliver air quality and allergen alerts to your watch.
- **Wake Lock** (`WAKE_LOCK`): To support periodic background updates for air quality checks.

These permissions are used strictly for app functionality and do not involve user tracking or external data harvesting.

## 🧾 Data Storage

The app stores public air quality measurements, station metadata, and pollen reports in a local on-device database (`airmetrics.db`) for caching and offline viewing. No personal data or user identifiers are stored in this database.

## 👤 User Rights

As we do not collect or store any personal data, there is no personal user data to access, modify, or delete.

## 👶 Children’s Privacy

This app is **not directed to children under the age of 13** and does not knowingly collect data from them.
